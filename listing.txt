Full description (max 4000 characters)
Take a slow breath and let the water flow.

Lotus Pond is a calm puzzle game about guiding a glowing stream through a pond of turning stones until every lotus blooms. Tap a stone to turn it, line up the channels, and watch the water spread outward in a soft glow. No rush, no noise, no pressure.

WHAT YOU WILL FIND
- 36 stages in each of four modes: Easy, Medium, Hard and Ultimate
- Six Challenge stages in every mode with new structures: twin springs, a heart-shaped pond, locked stones, three springs and a grand finale
- A new colour tone for every level, from Moonlit Teal to Ember Sky
- Pond shapes that change from level to level: octagons, diamonds, rings, crosses, hourglasses and hearts
- A Daily Pond: one fresh puzzle each day, with a streak to keep
- Star ratings, so you can come back and finish with fewer turns
- Undo and Hint buttons when you are stuck
- Gentle sounds and optional ambient music
- Works offline, no account, no ads, no data collected

HOW TO PLAY
Tap a stone to turn it. Connect the glowing spring to every lotus. Easy mode has no turn limit. Medium, Hard and Ultimate give you a number of turns, and Undo can give a turn back.

Made for quiet minutes between busy moments.
