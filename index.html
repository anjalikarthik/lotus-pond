How to play

  1. Tap a stone to turn it.
  2. Guide the glowing spring through the stones until the water reaches every lotus.
  3. Lotuses bloom when the water arrives. Bloom them all to finish.
  4. Stuck? Use Undo to take a move back, or Hint to see a stone that needs turning.
  5. Stones with a dot are locked. Medium and harder modes have limited turns.
