Lotus Pond Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026.

Lotus Pond is a puzzle game. It does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information.

  • No account: you do not need to sign in.
  • No ads and no analytics: the game contains no advertising and no tracking tools.
  • On your device only: your level progress, stars, daily streak and settings (sound, music, vibration) are saved in your device storage so the game can remember them. They never leave your device. You can erase them with Menu, then Reset progress, or by clearing the app data.
  • Permissions: the game may use the vibration feature of your phone for tap feedback. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.
  • Children: the game is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page.
